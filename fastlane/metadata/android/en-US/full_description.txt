Copy-paste, only better.

Copy some text on your computer, and it's already on your phone. Take a photo, and it's waiting for you on your Mac or PC, in full quality. Entre Nous is a clipboard shared across all your devices: Android, Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone, or just a browser.

No account to create. To add a device, scan a QR code or paste a link, and that's it. No sign-up, no email, no password.

Between us, and no one else. Everything is end-to-end encrypted: only your devices can read what you share, and the server only ever sees unreadable data. On the same Wi-Fi, what you share goes straight from one device to the other, without going through the internet. And nothing lingers: everything is deleted automatically, after 10 minutes to 7 days, as you choose.

In everyday use:
- From any app, "Share → Entre Nous", and it's on your other devices.
- Send a folder as easily as a file: see what's inside, download only what you want.
- Look at a photo, a PDF or a Markdown document without downloading it; play a video or an audio file while it's still arriving.
- Pin what you use often: it stays at the top, on all your devices.
- Show the Wi-Fi password to a guest as a QR code: their phone reads it, no app or account needed.
- A file to send to someone, or to ask them for? A simple link is enough.

Open source and private. No ads, no trackers. The code is public (AGPL license): anyone can check it, and you can host your own relay server with two commands.
